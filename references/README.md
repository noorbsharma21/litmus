# References

Work Noor likes that he did not make. Kept apart from the corpus on
purpose, because the corpus is evidence about him and this is not.

The corpus (43 pieces in `../images/`) is drawings, photographs and
three pages he built. Every claim in the engine was drawn out of that,
which means every claim was drawn out of pictures. Nothing has tested
whether those claims survive the jump to a built thing, a menu or a
poster. That assumption is load bearing and currently unexamined.

This folder is how it gets examined.

## What to put in

- `sites/` — screenshots or URLs of built things that are good
- `print/` — menus, posters, sleeves, books, anything off a screen

Either form works:

- **Screenshots**: png or jpg, any name. A full page beats a crop.
- **URLs**: one per line in `sites/urls.txt`. They get opened and read.

## Worth writing down, if it is quick

A line in `notes.md` saying what the thing is and what you take from
it. Not why it is good. What you would steal.

The engine already refuses the obvious failures, so a reference that
is merely competent teaches it nothing. The useful ones are the things
that are good in a way that is arguable: where somebody made a call
another good designer would not have made.

## What happens to them

They do not become rules. They get read the way the corpus was read,
tagged, and then held against the 13 claims to find out which ones
hold outside a sketchbook and which were only ever true of drawings.
